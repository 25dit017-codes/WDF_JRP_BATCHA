Welcome to StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple student portal developed as a Web Technology practical project. It provides students with one place to access their academic information.

Objectives

  • Provide easy access to student information.
  • Display attendance details.
  • View enrolled courses.
  • Check timetable.
  • View assignments.
  • Display semester results.
  • Publish college notices.
  • Maintain student profile.
